THE WORDS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

No jargon needed.

Reel
A short vertical video on Instagram.
Hook
The first second or two that makes people stop scrolling.
B-roll
Extra footage of details and action, used between main shots.
Cut
One edited version of a video, like a 15 or 30 second cut.
Vertical / 9:16
The tall shape that fills a phone screen.
Captions
Words on screen, so it works without sound.
Content day
One day of filming that gives you lots of short clips.
Brand film
A longer, cinematic video about who you are.

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

How much does a video cost?
A phone content day is $350. Camera video starts at $1,000, and brand films start at $1,500. Tell me where it will live and what it needs to do, and you'll get a clear price before anything is booked.
Do you come up with the idea?
Yes. Every project starts with the idea and the feeling, and I plan the shots around that. If you already have an idea, we build from yours.
Will I get vertical versions for Reels and TikTok?
Yes. One shoot gets cut for everywhere you need it: the main piece, vertical cuts, short teasers and loops.
Can I get photos from the same shoot?
Yes. I grab stills along the way, so one day of production covers more than one need. See photography →

Will it work with the sound off?
It's made to. Captions and pacing are planned so it works muted in the feed and with the sound up.
