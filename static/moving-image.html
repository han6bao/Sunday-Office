HOW IT WORKS

From idea to every cut.

  1. 01

    The idea

    What it should make people feel.

    We start with the feeling and where it will live. A 15-second reel and a homepage film are different jobs, so the idea comes first.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Shots, place, timing.

    A simple plan: the key shots, the location, the look, and how many cuts we'll need for each platform.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    Video and stills together.

    We film, and grab stills along the way, so one day of production covers more than one need.

  4. 04

    Edit

    Cut, color, sound.

    I edit, color and finish it, polished and commercial or rawer and stranger, whatever the project calls for.

  5. 05

    Cut for everywhere

    Ready to post.

    You get the main piece plus the versions you need: vertical, short teasers, loops and stills, so it keeps working long after it's posted.

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

How much does a video cost?
A phone content day is $350. Camera video starts at $700, and brand films start at $1,500. Tell me where it will live and what it needs to do, and you'll get a clear price before anything is booked.
Do you come up with the idea?
Yes. Every project starts with the idea and the feeling, and I plan the shots around that. If you already have an idea, we build from yours.
Will I get vertical versions for Reels and TikTok?
Yes. One shoot gets cut for everywhere you need it: the main piece, vertical cuts, short teasers and loops.
Can I get photos from the same shoot?
Yes. I grab stills along the way, so one day of production covers more than one need. See photography →
Will it work with the sound off?
It's made to. Captions and pacing are planned so it works muted in the feed and with the sound up.
