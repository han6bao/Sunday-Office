FOR MODELS

Need digitals?

Getting scouted, signing with an agency or applying for casting, digitals are the first thing they ask for. Here's what they're looking for, and what I make sure you have.

  • Your real face: clean skin, hair off the face, no heavy edit
  • Front, three-quarter, profile and full length
  • Simple fitted clothes, so your shape reads
  • Natural light that shows your true skin tone
  • Relaxed expression, eyes alive, no forced smile

I plan the shots, direct your posing and expression, keep the edit natural and help you pick the selects to send. Want a styled look too? We add it in the same session.
