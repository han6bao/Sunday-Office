MODEL DIGITALS · LOFT TACOMA · OCTOBER 11, 2026

Ellis.

MY FIRST CLIENT, SHOT ON A SUNDAY · TWO LOOKS · FULL GALLERY BELOW

FOR
Ellis, model
WHAT I DID
Location, styling, lighting, props, posing, editing and the final selects
WHERE
LOFT, Tacoma, WA

THE STORY

THE CIRCLE BACK

Ellis was my very first client when I opened my photography business. Years later, we shot his digitals on a Sunday, October 11, 2026, right as Sunday Office opened. Same person, new name on the door.

WHO HE IS

Ellis is a model. I've helped him walk the runway for Seattle Pride, and we keep making creative shoots together. These digitals are what he sends to agencies, scouts and casting.

TWO LOOKS

Look one is clean digitals: a plain white tee and wide jeans, so his face, body and proportions do the talking. Look two is styled: denim on denim, built to show range and personality.

WHAT I DID

THE PLAN

Scouted and booked the location, LOFT in Tacoma, and planned the shot list around what agencies ask for in digitals.

STYLING

Helped style both looks, from the denim layering to the details, and fixed hair and clothes between every setup.

LIGHT + PROPS

Found the best natural light in the loft and chose the props: the brick wall, the velvet chaise and the full-length mirror.

POSING + EXPRESSION

Directed every pose and kept an eye on the small things: hands, posture, chin, eyes. Digitals live or die on expression, so we kept going until it was right.

THE EDIT

A natural edit, true to his skin and the room. Digitals need to look like the person who walks in the door.

THE CULL

After the shoot we went to a coffee shop in Tacoma and went through every frame together to pick the strongest ones.

FOR MODELS

Getting scouted or signing with an agency? Digitals are the first thing they ask for: simple, honest photos that show exactly how you look today. I plan the shot list agencies expect, guide your posing and expression, and help you pick the selects, so you send the strongest set you can. Want a styled look too? We can add one in the same session.

I work with creatives, aspiring models and artists to build the vision with them, not just take the picture.
