HER STORY

"I know what it feels like to be on the other side of the aisle."

Planning a wedding showed Jazmin just how many details sit behind one beautiful day. Her business grew from that: a love of bringing the details together, so the people at the center can actually enjoy what they planned.

Whether a couple needs her for a few final pieces or from beginning to end, she wants them to feel like someone is genuinely in their corner.

IN HER WORDS, FROM HER ABOUT PAGE

HOW I APPROACHED IT

Calm first. Then romantic.

Calm

Couples arrive carrying a lot: venues, vendors, families, timelines. If the brand felt frantic, nobody would trust her with the day.

For who books

Light, airy and romantic: arches, botanicals, creams and greens, script flourishes. It should feel like the wedding they're picturing.

Show first

We'd never worked together, so I built her a clickable mockup before asking for anything. She could see it and say yes.

THE BRAND
