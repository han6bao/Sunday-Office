Selaras Haus.

BY ANGIE TIARA · SKIN, SCALP + MAKEUP BOUTIQUE · TACOMA · INTERIOR PHOTOS BY HANA

THE OWNER

Selaras Haus is a skin, scalp and makeup boutique by Angie Tiara, a makeup artist and licensed esthetician. She does makeup, facials and head spa treatments: scalp analysis, double wash, steam, exfoliation and a warm herbal rinse people drive over for.

THE STUDIO

Her brand-new space at 1001 Pacific Ave in downtown Tacoma. The photos basically sell the place: the clean treatment room, the gold water arch over the sink, the gold-framed mirror at the station, hydrangeas in the corner. It's a calm, warm room that matches her work.

THE FEEL

Natural, soft and warm. That's how Angie wants people to feel the moment they walk in, so that's how I shot it.
