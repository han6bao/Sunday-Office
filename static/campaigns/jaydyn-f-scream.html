THE ARTIST

Jaydyn F. is part of Seattle's underground rap scene. He makes music, throws shows and brings local artists, touring names and friends into the same room. SCREAM is his new single, and he produced the video himself.

THE DIRECTOR · REAL WORLD JAKE

Real World Jake (@realwrldjake) is a music video director and editor managed by Zero Zero MGMT. He's worked with artists like Bhad Bhabie, TEZZUS and Lil Tracy. He shot and edited SCREAM, and shared creative direction with Jaydyn. We're all Seattle creators.

THE ROLLOUT

The photos became the release.

Before the drop, Jaydyn had a whole gallery of assets to tease the song. He posted snippets on his stories with the photos and behind the scenes clips, so people saw the new era before they heard it.

The photos kept going after that. He used them for his profile pictures and backgrounds on Spotify and Apple Music, changing them as the release went on. This blue frame became his YouTube profile picture.
