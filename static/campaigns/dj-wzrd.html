DJ WZRD.

AT CULTURA · CAPITOL HILL · PHOTOS BY HANA

THE DJ

DJ WZRD is a world-touring DJ and producer who plays music across borders (Mexico, Los Angeles, Seattle, Canada, Sacramento) and builds his sets on instinct: "If I don't understand the language of a song, I'll still play it if it has a good vibe." He's performed at the 10th Anniversary of EDC Mexico, holds a residency at Six Flags Mexico, appears on Pitbull's Globalization (Ritmolucion) on SiriusXM and on TV Azteca, and his YouTube has pulled over 16 million views. He wears white frames and a gold VZZRD chain, and he reads a crowd better than almost anyone.

THE VENUE · CULTURA

Cultura Seattle calls itself "Seattle's #1 Latin club," and it sits in the heart of Capitol Hill at 916 E Pike St. Reggaeton, dembow, merengue and cumbia nights with international DJs, VIP tables, bottle service, and lasers through the smoke. This was one of those nights. It sold out, and people still talk about it.

THE NIGHT

Pink and purple light, a packed room, and one guy in white frames up above it all with his hands on the decks, mic pulled in, chain catching the strobe. The halo shot sums up the whole night for me. The room shot underneath backs it up: packed, loud, nowhere to move.

THE PROMO

He reposted the shot, so the whole night went back out on his feed: the booth, the crowd, the chain. It didn't need a caption. That's what good promo photos are for.
