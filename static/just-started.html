STAGE 01 OF 07

Make it official

Do these first. Everything else depends on them.

  1. Choose your business structure

    Sole proprietor, LLC or corporation. Free advisors can help you decide.

    Free advising in Washington →

  2. Register with the state

    LLCs and corporations file with the Washington Secretary of State.

    Where to file →

  3. Get your business license

    Through the Department of Revenue's Business Licensing Service, plus your city license if you have one.

    License links →

  4. Open a business bank account

    Keep business money separate from day one. It makes taxes much easier.

  5. Get an EIN if you need one

    Free from the IRS. You'll need it to hire or for some bank accounts.

    Where to get it →
