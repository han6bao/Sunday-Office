PRODUCT LAUNCHES · SEATTLE

Launching a product, a drop or a new menu.

A launch only happens once. The photos, video and posts around it decide whether people notice. Here's how to plan it so it lands.

SOUND FAMILIAR?

  • You have a launch date but no plan for the content
  • Your product photos look like everyone else's
  • You want it to feel like an event

WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Pick one idea

    One feeling, one look, one line people remember. Every photo and post comes from it.

  2. 02

    Shoot it all in one day

    Plan photos and short videos together so they match and you're not booking three shoots.

  3. 03

    Tease, launch, follow up

    A few posts before, a strong launch day and content after. Most launches stop too early.

  4. 04

    Put it where people search

    Update your website and Google listing so new people can find it, not just followers.

How far ahead should I plan a launch?
Three to four weeks is comfortable. That leaves time to plan, shoot, edit and schedule a few teaser posts.

Yes. Campaign shoots are planned as one, so the stills and the video feel like the same world.
