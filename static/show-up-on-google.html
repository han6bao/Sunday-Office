GET FOUND ON GOOGLE · SEATTLE

Showing up on Google.

When someone nearby searches for what you do, you want to be there. Most of it is free and simple once you know what to fill in.

SOUND FAMILIAR?

  • You don't appear when people search your service nearby
  • Your Google listing has no photos
  • Your website has no page titles or descriptions

WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Claim your Google Business Profile

    Fill out every field: hours, services, area, booking link. Add at least 10 real photos.

  2. 02

    Collect reviews steadily

    A few new reviews every month beats a burst once a year. Reply to every one.

  3. 03

    Give Google a website to read

    Clear page titles, your city and service in the words, fast on phones.

  4. 04

    Be consistent everywhere

    Same name, address and phone on Google, Instagram, Yelp and your site.

  5. 05

    Keep it fresh

    Post updates and new photos to your profile. Active listings get noticed.
