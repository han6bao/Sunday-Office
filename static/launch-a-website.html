NEW WEBSITES · SEATTLE

Launching your first website.

Most small businesses only need a few pages that do their job well. Here's what those pages are, and how to get one live without the headache.

SOUND FAMILIAR?

  • People keep asking for your prices and hours in DMs
  • You use a link-in-bio as your website
  • You don't show up when people search your name

WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Start with four pages

    Home, services, about and contact. Add a gallery or menu if your work is visual.

  2. 02

    Make booking obvious

    One clear button on every page. Connect your booking tool, payment links and maps.

  3. 03

    Write for your customer

    What you do, who it's for, what it costs to start and what happens next. Plain words win.

  4. 04

    Set it up for Google

    Page titles, descriptions and local search basics, so people near you can find it.
