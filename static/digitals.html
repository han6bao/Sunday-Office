QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

What are digitals?
Simple, natural photos that show agencies exactly what you look like today. No heavy makeup, no styling tricks and no reshaping. Some people call them polaroids.
Do you retouch them?
Lightly. I clean up small things like a stray hair or a bit of dust, but I never change your face, your skin or your shape. That's the point of digitals.
Where do we shoot?
In a studio or on location with good natural light, your choice. Ellis's digitals were shot at LOFT in Tacoma. See them →
How fast do I get them?
A sneak peek within 48 hours and the full gallery in about two weeks. We pick your selects together.
2 MORE QUESTIONS +
Can you help me get signed?
I can't promise that, and anyone who does isn't being honest. What I can promise is digitals that show you clearly and help you put your best foot forward.
Do I need makeup or a stylist?
No. Clean, moisturized skin is all you need. If you want a styled look after the digitals, we can plan that together.
