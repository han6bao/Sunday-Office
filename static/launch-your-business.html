WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Make it official

    Register your business with the state and get your licenses sorted first. My Washington resources page lists every office and link.

  2. 02

    Build the brand once

    A logo, colors, fonts and the way you talk. Do it before the website and the flyers, so everything matches from day one.

  3. 03

    Give people a home base

    A simple site with what you do, who it's for and a way to book. Even one good page beats a link-in-bio.

  4. 04

    Show the real thing

    Real photos of you, your space and your work. People trust what they can see.

  5. 05

    Tell people you're open

    A Google Business Profile, a launch post and a few weeks of content planned ahead.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Quick answers.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

What should I do first when starting a business?
Register it and get licensed, then build your brand. Your brand decides how your website, photos and posts look, so it saves you from redoing things later.
Do I need a website if I have Instagram?
Yes, if you want to be found on Google. Instagram helps people who already follow you. A website helps people searching for what you do.
Can you do the brand, website and photos together?
Yes. That's what Sunday Office is built for: one person, one plan, from the logo to the last post.
