WASHINGTON SMALL BUSINESS RESOURCES

Starting or growing a business in Washington.

The offices, free advisors and local groups worth knowing, in one place. Most of them are free. Rules change, so always check the official page for the latest.

MAKE IT OFFICIAL

Register and get licensed.

Small Business Guide: opening checklist ↗Washington's step-by-step list of what to do before you open.Secretary of State: form your LLC or corporation ↗File here first if you're not a sole proprietor. You'll get a UBI number.Department of Revenue: apply for a business license ↗The Business Licensing Service, with a wizard that tells you which endorsements you need.City of Seattle: business license tax certificate ↗Needed if you do business in Seattle. Other cities have their own.IRS: get an EIN ↗Free federal tax ID, needed to hire or for some bank accounts.

FREE HELP

Advisors who don't charge.

Washington SBDC ↗No-cost, confidential one-on-one business advising across the state.Greater Seattle SCORE ↗Free mentoring, workshops and tools from volunteer business mentors.SBA Seattle District Office ↗Federal loans, programs and local events for small businesses.Seattle Office of Economic Development ↗City programs, guides and support for Seattle small businesses.Seattle Public Library: business help ↗Free databases, workshops and a list of local business organizations.

FUNDING + CERTIFICATION

Money and recognition.

OMWBE certification ↗Washington's office for minority, women and veteran-owned business certification.Rainier Valley Community Development Fund ↗Community lender with business loans and technical help.IRC Center for Economic Opportunity ↗Small-dollar business loans for New Americans and credit-thin borrowers.King's Dream: Community Capital program ↗Free consulting and coaching for Black, Latine and Tribal-owned businesses in King, Pierce and Snohomish counties.

COMMUNITY

People to know.

Greater Seattle Business Association (GSBA) ↗An LGBTQ chamber of commerce with networking and development.Seattle Made ↗Connects local makers and manufacturers with resources and promotion.Friends of Little Saigon ↗Support for small businesses in Seattle's Little Saigon.Growing Contigo ↗Bilingual consulting for Spanish-speaking and immigrant-owned businesses.

GET FOUND

Free listings every business should claim.

Google Business Profile ↗Shows you on Google Search and Maps. Free, and the biggest local win.Apple Business Connect ↗Your listing on Apple Maps, for everyone searching on an iPhone.Yelp for Business ↗Claim your page so your hours, photos and replies are right.

This page is a starting point, not legal or tax advice. For your situation, talk to a free advisor at the SBDC or SCORE.
