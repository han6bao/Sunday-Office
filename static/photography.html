QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

How much does a shoot cost?
Headshots start at $150 (see sessions). Brand and creative shoots start at $550, and events are $150 an hour with a two-hour minimum. You'll know the final number before anything is booked.
I've never been photographed. Is that okay?
Most people haven't. I direct you the whole time, so you look like yourself, not stiff.
Where do we shoot?
Wherever suits the photos: your space, a location we pick together, or somewhere that fits the look. We decide when we plan.
Can you do video on the same day?
Yes. Photos and video can come from the same shoot, so one day covers more. See moving image →
