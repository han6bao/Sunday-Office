A GUIDE · SEATTLE + TACOMA

Where to get digitals and headshots in Seattle and Tacoma.

One of my favorite places is LOFT in Tacoma. Here's a real shoot there with Ellis, my very first client, and everything the space has that made it easy.

THE SHOOT

One hour, two outfits.

I booked a one-hour session and we still had time for two looks: his basic digitals, and a slightly more styled denim look. Booking was smooth and parking was easy.

We didn't need a backdrop. The painted brick wall, white floor and bright natural light did everything, so there was no extra lighting or flash at all. The big mirror helped with touch-ups, and we used the chaise, the rack and the steamer between looks.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Where can I get model digitals in Seattle or Tacoma?
Any space with big windows and plain walls works. LOFT in Tacoma is one I like: natural light, white brick and a white floor, so digitals come out clean without extra lighting.
Where can I get headshots in Seattle or Tacoma?
Headshots can be shot in a studio like LOFT, at your workplace or outside. A bright, quiet space with a plain background is the easiest way to get a clean, natural headshot.
How long do digitals take?
Ellis's session was booked for one hour, and we had time for his digitals and a second styled outfit.
Do I need a backdrop for digitals?
Usually not. A plain light wall and good daylight are enough, which is what we used at LOFT.
Who books the studio?
I can book and plan the space for you, or shoot at a location you already have in mind.
