WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Look around your field

    Find five businesses like yours that you admire. Notice the colors, words and photos they all share, then find what makes you different.

  2. 02

    Pick a feeling, not a style

    Calm, bold, playful, luxurious. One feeling guides every choice after it.

  3. 03

    Make it recognizable

    One color, one type style and one kind of photo, used every single time.

  4. 04

    Say it your way

    The words matter as much as the look. Write like you talk to your favorite client.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

Quick answers.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

How do I make my brand stand out?
Find what everyone in your industry does the same, then pick one feeling and commit to it everywhere: logo, colors, photos and words.
Do I need a new logo to stand out?
Not always. Often a brand kit built around your current logo, plus better photos, does more than a new mark.
What is world building?
It's making everything around your logo feel like one place: the colors, the photos, the site and the posts, so people recognize you before they read your name.
