BEFORE YOUR SESSION

Four things to know.

01

Wear what feels like you

Solid colors, softer necklines, and clothes you already feel good in. When you're comfortable it shows, so keep the outfit simple and let your face be the focus.

02

Light is the shortcut

Flat light ruins most headshots. I set the light for your best angles before you even start posing. You just show up.

03

Know what it's for

LinkedIn, a creative brand, social media or something more artistic. Each one needs a different feel and crop, so tell me what it's for and I'll shoot for that.

04

Nervous? that's normal

No idea how to pose? That's fine. You tell me what the photo is for and I'll handle the rest. First-timers usually leave surprised by how good they look.
