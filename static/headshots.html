Gallery

Editing
Color, light and cleanup on each finished photo.
Retouching

Where you're allowed to use the photos. For brand shoots, that's your own channels.
Golden hour
The soft light just after sunrise or before sunset.

Behind the scenes. Great for social posts.

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

How many photos do I get?
Standard includes 1 finished photo. More looks includes 5. Personal branding comes with a bigger gallery, and you'll know the exact number when you book.
Where do we shoot?
We pick the spot together, based on the look you want and where the photos will be used.
What should I wear?
Solid colors, softer necklines and clothes you already feel good in. Not sure? Bring a few options and we'll choose together on the day.
I've never had headshots taken. Will I be awkward?
Most people feel that way at first. I direct you the whole time, from where to stand to how to tilt your chin, so all you have to do is show up.
