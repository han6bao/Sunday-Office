PROMOTION · CONTENT + CAMPAIGNS

Promoting your business.

Promotion works best when you have something worth sharing and a simple plan for where it goes. Here's how to get both.

SOUND FAMILIAR?

  • You post, but it doesn't go anywhere
  • You have news but no photos to show it
  • You're not sure which platform matters

WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Have a reason to talk

    A launch, a season, a new service or a story. Promotion needs a hook.

  2. 02

    Make it worth sharing

    Strong photos and short videos travel further than graphics with a lot of words.

  3. 03

    Choose two places

    Most local businesses do best on Instagram and Google. Do two well instead of five badly.

  4. 04

    Let others share it

    Tag partners, venues and the people in it. My photo of Nine Vicious was picked up by a music news page.
