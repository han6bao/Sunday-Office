WEBSITE WORDS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

No tech talk needed.

Domain
Your address online, like yourname.com. You own it and renew it once a year for a small fee.
Hosting
Where your site lives so it loads for people. Think of it as rent for the site.
SEO
The setup that helps Google understand what you do and where, so people searching can find you.
Booking link
The tool people use to book you, like Square or Vagaro. Your site sends people straight to it.
Landing page
One focused page, usually for one offer or one event, with one button.
Mobile-first
Designed for phones before computers, because that's where most people will see it.
Form
The boxes people fill in to reach you. Their message lands in your email.
Analytics
A simple count of who visits, from where, and what they click.

I already have a site. Do we have to start over?
Not unless it makes sense. We can fix what's broken, refresh the look, swap in better photos or tighten your portfolio. That's quoted separately from a full build.

It's the same site. The SEO version adds the setup that helps Google understand you: page titles, descriptions, local Seattle search and a sitemap.

Some things you own, like your domain name, have their own small costs. I'll walk you through each one before we start, so nothing comes as a surprise.
