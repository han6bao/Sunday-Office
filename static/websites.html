QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

Do I need my words and photos ready?
No. Writing the words with you is part of every site, and we choose the photos together. If you need new photos, I can shoot those too.
How long does it take?
It depends on how many pages you need and how quickly we gather your words and photos. You'll get a timeline with your price before anything starts.
I'm not techy. Is that a problem?
Not at all. I handle the tech and walk you through anything you need to do on your end, in plain words.
Do I need a logo first?
No. If you don't have one yet, we can start with the brand. Build a World →
4 MORE QUESTIONS +
I already have a site. Do we have to start over?
Not unless it makes sense. We can fix what's broken, refresh the look, swap in better photos or tighten your portfolio. That's quoted separately from a full build.
What's the difference between the simple site and the SEO one?
It's the same site. The SEO version adds the setup that helps Google understand you: page titles, descriptions, local Seattle search and a sitemap.
Will there be other costs?
Some things you own, like your domain name, have their own small costs. I'll walk you through each one before we start, so nothing comes as a surprise.
What if I need changes after launch?
Every site comes with one round of small changes after launch, free. After that, updates are $70 each. Bigger changes get their own quote.
