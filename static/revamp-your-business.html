A FREE GUIDE · BUSINESS MAKEOVERS

I want to revamp my business.

You're up and running, but the look, the site or the content doesn't match where the business is now. Go through this before you spend anything, so you fix what actually matters.

STAGE 01

Take an honest look

Before changing anything, see what customers see.

  1. Search your business on Google

    What shows up? Is your info right? Are there photos and reviews?

  2. Open your website on your phone

    Can you find the booking button in three seconds?

  3. Scroll your last 12 posts

    Do they look like the same business? Would you follow it?

  4. Ask three clients why they chose you

    Their words are often better than anything you'd write.

STAGE 02

Decide: refresh or rebuild

Not everything needs to start over.

  1. Keep what people recognize

    If customers know your logo, a brand kit built around it may be enough.

    Build a World →

  2. Rebuild what's holding you back

    A site that fights you or a look that no longer fits is worth replacing.

    Improving your website →

  3. Write down what's changed

    New services, new customers, higher prices. Your brand should catch up.

STAGE 03

Refresh the look

The fastest way to look like the business you are now.

  1. Update your colors, fonts and voice

    Small changes, used consistently, feel like a big upgrade.

    Creative branding →

  2. Replace old and stock photos

    New photos change a website and feed faster than anything else.

    Photography →

  3. New headshots

    If your photo is from a few years ago, it's time.

    Headshots →

STAGE 04

Fix the website

Where most people decide whether to book.

  1. Cut the words in half

    What you do, who it's for, how to book. The rest goes lower or goes away.

  2. Make booking one tap

    Same button on every page, connected to your booking tool.

  3. Check speed and phones

    Slow or messy on a phone means visitors leave.

  4. Update your Google profile to match

    New photos, hours, services and links.

    Showing up on Google →

STAGE 05

Reset your content

Show people the new version of you.

  1. Plan a relaunch moment

    A post or short series that says what's new.

  2. Batch a month of content

    One content day can fill weeks of posts and Reels.

    Social + Content →

  3. Get templates in the new look

    So everything you post from now on matches.

    Social packs →

STAGE 06

Keep it up

A revamp lasts when someone looks after it.

  1. Refresh photos each season

    A few times a year keeps things from going stale.

  2. Keep asking for reviews

    A few every month beats a burst once a year.

  3. Check your site every month

    Or let someone handle updates and reports for you.
