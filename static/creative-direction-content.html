CREATIVE NOTES

A few things I believe about content.

01 / 12
01

Pattern interruption

If everything in the feed looks the same, give the eye something unexpected. Change the framing, opening line, pace, crop, sound, or format.

02

The first line matters

“Here are our services” says less than “You probably don’t need everything we offer.” Give people a reason to keep reading.

03

Make it feel like you

Trends can be useful, but copying one exactly usually isn't. Take the format and make it your own.

04

One idea, multiple lives

A 20-minute conversation can become a long-form video, six short clips, a quote carousel, stories, stills, and a follow-up post.

05

Emotion is information

Funny, nostalgic, hopeful, curious, comforting, urgent. Decide how you want someone to feel before you decide what the post looks like.

06

The title is part of the design

The right words can stop someone scrolling before the picture does. Hooks, captions and on-screen text deserve as much thought as the visuals.

07

Not everything needs to sell

Some posts help people recognize you, some build trust, some are just fun, and some make sales. A good feed needs a mix.

08

The grid is the story

People visit your profile, not just one post. Plan how the whole feed and your highlights look together, not just each picture.

09

Consistency outranks perfection

Posting regularly in a voice people recognize does more than one perfect post. Keep showing up and get better as you go.

10

Sound on, sound off

Lots of people watch on mute with captions, and lots listen closely. Make it work both ways.

11

The save is the new like

Saves and shares show that people actually valued something. Give them something worth keeping, like a list, a tip or a feeling.

12

Mood before format

Pick the feeling first, then the format. The same idea could be a story, a reel, a carousel or a photo.

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

Either. Some people start with the audit and strategy session and run with it. Others want content every month, which is what the monthly plans are for. Monthly plans start with a 3-month commitment.
Which platforms do you work with?
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn. Starter covers one platform, Growth covers two plus Facebook, and Full covers all four, including a LinkedIn plan.
Do I get to approve things first?
Always. Every template and every piece of the plan is yours to approve before anything goes out.
What happens to the audit price if I sign up monthly?
It comes off your first month. Growth and Full include the audit free in month one.
Do you post for me?
If you want. Posting and rollout can be part of it, or I hand everything over ready for you to post.
What do you need from me?
A conversation about your business and who you want to reach, and access to whatever you already have. We figure out the rest together.
How do we know it's working?
We watch what lands: saves, shares, comments and who reaches out. The next round of content gets better because of it.
