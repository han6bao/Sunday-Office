QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form, or email hello@sundayoffice.agency.

Is this a one-time thing or monthly?
Either. Some people start with the audit and strategy session and run with it. Others want content every month, which is what the monthly plans are for. Monthly plans start with a 3-month commitment. After that, plans continue month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days notice.
Which platforms do you work with?
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn. Starter covers one platform, Growth covers two plus Facebook, and Full covers all four, including a LinkedIn plan.
Do I get to approve things first?
Always. Every template and every piece of the plan is yours to approve before anything goes out.
What happens to the audit price if I sign up monthly?
It comes off your first month. Growth and Full include the audit free in month one.
5 MORE QUESTIONS +
Do you post for me?
On Full, I post for you. On Starter and Growth, I hand everything over ready to post and you press publish.
What do you need from me?
A conversation about your business and who you want to reach, and access to whatever you already have. We figure out the rest together.
How do we know it's working?
We watch what lands: saves, shares, comments and who reaches out. The next round of content gets better because of it.
Can you shoot the content too?
Yes. Photos, video, graphics and the words on top can all come from me, so it looks like it belongs together. Photography →
Can I switch plans later?
You can upgrade anytime. Downgrades take effect at the end of a billing month once the first 3 months are done.
