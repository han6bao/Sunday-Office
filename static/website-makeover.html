Improving the website you already have.

Your site works, sort of. It looks dated, it's slow on phones or nobody books from it. You don't always have to start over.

SOUND FAMILIAR?

  • It looks great on a laptop and messy on a phone
  • Visitors leave without booking
  • You're embarrassed to send people the link

WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

Do these, in this order.

  1. 01

    Check it on your phone

    Most of your visitors are on one. If the button is hard to find there, fix that first.

  2. 02

    Swap the photos

    Old or stock photos make a good business look generic. New photos change a site faster than anything.

  3. 03

    Cut the words in half

    Say what you do, who it's for and how to book. Everything else goes lower or goes away.

  4. 04

    Decide: refresh or rebuild

    If the platform is fighting you, a rebuild costs less than patching it forever.
