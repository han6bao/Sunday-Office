What's included in a brand?
A logo suite, color palette, fonts, your voice and a guide. Bigger brands add textures, templates, print pieces and photos.
How is branding different from a logo?
A logo is one mark. A brand is everything around it: how you look, sound and feel everywhere people find you.
Can I edit my brand templates myself?
Yes. Your brand is set up in Canva with templates, and there's a step-by-step guide if you've never used it.
