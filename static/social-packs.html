WHAT MAKES A GOOD PHOTO

  1. Bright. Daylight by a window beats overhead lights.
  2. Sharp. Tap your phone screen to focus before you shoot.
  3. Right way up. Vertical for stories, vertical or square for posts.
  4. Yours. Your space, your work, your people, your product.

WHAT CANVA IS

A free design app, made for everyone.

Canva is where your social pack lives. Think of it like a photo editor where everything is already designed for you. You just change the picture and the words.

It's free to start

Canva has a free plan, and your social pack is built to work on it. Canva Pro adds extras like one-click resizing and scheduling, but you don't need it.

It works anywhere

Use it in your web browser at canva.com, or download the Canva app on your phone, tablet or computer. Your designs follow you everywhere you log in.

It's made for people who aren't designers

Everything is click, drag and type. If you can post a story, you can use Canva.

STEP BY STEP

Your first post, start to finish.

0 of 8 done. Tap a step when you finish it.

  1. Make a free Canva account

    Go to canva.com and click Sign up. Use your Google account, Apple ID or email. Use the email you want your business designs saved under.

    Already have Canva? Just log in. Skip to step 2.

  2. Open your social pack link

    I'll send you one link for your whole pack. Click it while you're logged in to Canva.

    Save the link somewhere safe, like your notes. You can always use it to get a fresh copy.

  3. Make your own copy

    Click Use template for new design. This makes a copy that's yours to change. The original stays untouched.

    Messed something up? Open the link again and you get a clean copy.

  4. Add your photos

    On the left side, click Uploads, then Upload files, and pick your photos.

    Shooting with me? Your photos are already in. Skip ahead.

  5. Swap a photo

    Drag a photo from Uploads right onto the photo in the design. It snaps into place. Double-click it to move or zoom it inside the frame.

  6. Change the words

    Double-click any text to type over it. Keep the same font and color, they're already set to your brand.

    If the text gets too long for the box, shorten it before you shrink it.

  7. Download it

    Click Share at the top right, then Download. Choose PNG for posts and stories, or MP4 if it moves. Pick just the pages you want.

  8. Post it

    Upload the file to Instagram, Facebook or TikTok like any other photo. Paste in a caption from your caption guide.

    That's it. Next week, duplicate the page and change the photo and words.

KEEP IT LOOKING LIKE YOU

A few simple rules.

Do

  • Keep your fonts and colors. They're what makes it look like you
  • Use one idea per post
  • Use your own bright, sharp photos
  • Duplicate a page before you change it, so you keep the original

Skip

  • Stretch or recolor your logo
  • Add a new font for fun
  • Fill every empty space. Space is part of the design
  • Use photos saved from Google. They're often blurry, and they're not yours to use

CANVA WORDS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Template
A design that's already made, so you only change the photo and words.

A photo spot in a design. Drop a picture on it and it fills the shape.
Element
Anything on the page: a shape, a line, a sticker, a photo.

Where your own photos and videos live inside Canva.

Make a copy of a page, so you can change one and keep the other.

The best file type for posts and stories. Sharp and clean.
MP4
The file type for anything that moves, like a Reel cover video.

The middle of a story, away from the top and bottom where Instagram puts its buttons.

The small round icon above your posts that holds saved stories.
