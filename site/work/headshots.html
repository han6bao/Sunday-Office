How I work

Light first, then you.

Light comes first: It shapes your face, sets the mood, and makes you look like your favorite version of yourself.

You don't need to pose: I'll tell you where to stand, how to move, and talk you through it the whole time.

Real over perfect: A real smile beats a perfect one. We go for the feeling first and sort out the technical side after.

Same care, every time: Your hundredth shoot or your first, you get the same care and the same light.

Sessions

Pick what fits.

Standard: One clean headshot, quick and easy. One look, one finished photo, direction the whole time.

More looks: Two outfits and five finished photos, so you have the right photo for every profile and platform.

Personal branding: A planning call and moodboard, more looks and setups, and a bigger gallery made for your website and socials.

Before you come

A few easy things.

Wear what feels like you: Solid colors, softer necklines, and clothes you already feel good in. When you're comfortable it shows.

Know what it's for: LinkedIn, a creative brand, social media or something more artistic. Each one needs a different feel and crop, so tell me and I'll shoot for that.

Nervous? That's normal: Most people feel that way at first. I direct you the whole time, so all you have to do is show up.

Questions

Good to know.

Where do we shoot? We pick the spot together, based on the look you want and where the photos will be used.

Can you shoot my whole team? Yes. Tell me how many people and where the photos will live, and I'll plan the day so everyone matches.

Will you share my photos? Usually yes, on my site and Instagram. If you'd rather keep them private, just tell me.
