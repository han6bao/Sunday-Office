THE ARTIST

Still Different is an MC with deep roots in the 206 hip-hop scene. He has used that name since his first project in 2013. He is most at home performing live.

THE GEAR · ETC TACOMA

For this shoot he wore ETC, a streetwear label from Tacoma. The jacket says ETC · 1996 OFFICIAL DREAM on the sleeve, TACOMA across the front, and has a big red C on the back.

ABOUT ETC

Tacoma, worn on the chest.

ETC (eTc Tacoma) is a streetwear label and shop in downtown Tacoma, started in 2014 by Tacoma natives Umi Wagoner and Perris Wright. A few years in, they stopped carrying other brands and now make only their own: limited runs with Mount Rainier, the Tacoma Dome and local team colors on them.

The shop is also a home base for Tacoma’s creative scene, with art shows, open mics, listening parties and pop-ups. They have made collaborations with the Tacoma Rainiers, the Seattle Kraken and the Washington State Fair.

WHY TACOMA MATTERS

Tacoma is the port town just south of Seattle. It has its own hip-hop, streetwear and DIY show scene. Tacoma is his hometown, and the jacket puts the name right across his chest.
