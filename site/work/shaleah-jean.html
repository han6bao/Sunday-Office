The artist

A new name with a famous family.

Shaleah Jean is a Seattle R&B singer-songwriter and the granddaughter of funk legend Bootsy Collins. She is building a sound of her own: contemporary R&B with honest, conversational writing.

The music

"Right Back."

Her single "Right Back" is about knowing your worth and not going back to old patterns. It has been written up by Sway's Universe and Hip Hop United, and she talked about her legacy and goals on Majic 107.5 in Atlanta.

The pictures

Photos made to promote her.

These are the photos she used for her promotion. They are bright, warm and close, with room for her braids, her name necklace and her personality to come through. Her name has been getting around since she started posting them.
