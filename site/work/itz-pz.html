The artist

Who he is.

Itz Pz (Peezy, if you know him) is a Seattle rapper who makes music with his group and team in the ReelClip circle. His releases include Life of Pz, EFFORTLESS and the YN TAKEOVER EP.

The pictures

Promo photos that took on a life of their own.

I shot him in the paisley session before the deal. He used the pictures for his socials and promotions, and then a lot of people started using these photos as their own profile pictures on Twitter and other apps.

The signing

Right before Empire.

A couple of months after the shoot, he signed to Empire Records, the label that released Kendrick Lamar's Section.80. These photos are from right before that, at the end of his independent run.

He is out now with the YN TAKEOVER Pt. 1 EP (2026), his first record since signing with Empire.

The brand · Paradice Worldwide

Taking chances.

Paradice Worldwide is a streetwear label from South Seattle, run by Ari Glass and Harry "Clean" out of 2919 Rainier Ave S. Their motto is "TAKING CHANCES.": heavyweight pattern work, dice and destiny references, and the paisley print that became their signature. The store sells the clothing and hosts creative work in the same space.

The campaign

One shoot, two wins.

One artist's pictures can promote him, the brand he's wearing and the circle behind him. He got promo photos, and Paradice got campaign content: their pieces worn by an artist people follow, right as his career took off.

My thought process

Light, edit, why.

The light: I helped set up the lighting for this one: straight studio, hard even light against a white wall. That setup shows the print and his face clearly.

The edit: I edited it dark and gritty, with high saturation and a lo-fi grain that keeps the clothes looking real. There was no set dressing, so the paisley print does most of the work.

The circle

Seattle, working together.

The session comes out of the same creative circle as Soniq Reign and ReelClip: artists, labels and studios around Seattle who keep working together from one shoot to the next.

For artists + clothing brands

One shoot can cover both.

If you're an artist, these are photos you can promote yourself with. If you're a clothing brand, this shows your pieces worn by people your customers follow.
