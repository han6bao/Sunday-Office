# SUNDAY OFFICE — design brief

**Design read** — An independent creative agency (Seattle / Tacoma) built as a
beautifully art-directed publication: a photography-led creative office, an
independent magazine and an archival contact sheet translated into a scrollable
editorial site. Classic, cultured, feminine-intelligent, restrained, tactile,
slightly nostalgic, contemporary. Not a photographer portfolio, not a corporate
agency site, not a scrapbook.

**Concept spine** — "The printed lead-sheet of the office." The site behaves
like leaves of an archival contact sheet / agency dossier: cream laid paper,
hairline rules locking sections, file numbers, one signature darkroom frame
that holds the work. Photography is the hero and the brand is the frame.

**Delivery tier** — `editorial` (typography + imagery + bespoke chrome,
micro-motion only). Motion is motivated parallax drift and the dark-framed
horizontal gallery, not novelty.

**Palette (locked — user's own brand board)**
  - cream `#F3EDE2` — dominant ground, warm paper white
  - deep navy `#0F1E33` — type, rules, section fields
  - muted slate `#547094` — secondary tone
  - antique gold `#C8A062` — wordmark + sun (old print / faded gilding)
  - oxblood red `#B23A2E` — 福祿壽 / 創作 seals, selected links, tiny rules
  - warm near-black `#1B1712` — the dark portfolio frame
  - hairline sepia `#8C7B66` — dividing rules on cream (so lines read printed)

**Locked type**
  - Display serif: **Bodoni Moda** (tall, high-contrast — masthead pressure)
  - Script: **Great Vibes** (formal — used sparingly, a word or two)
  - Sans: **Inter** (functional)
  - Mono: **IBM Plex Mono** (file numbers, archive, captions)
  - CJK calligraphy: **Zhi Mang Xing** (福祿壽 / 創作 seals)

**Animation mode: `non-animated`** — user chose an animated/parallax feel but
explicitly forbade all generated photography and video (film is impossible
without photos), so parallax + editorial motion are delivered on the plain
scaffold instead of a scroll-scrub film. Motivated parallax drift (sun, motifs,
gallery) is the Tier-1 feel, not a passive loop.

**Section plan** (one-orientation families, repeated only with different grids)
  01 Opening hero — editorial masthead + sun, placeholders begin below
  02 WHO WE ARE — manifesto ("Strategy in mind. Culture at heart. Story in
     everything.") + the 福祿壽 stamp + Vietnamese phrase
  03 CURRENT WORK — THE DARK FRAME: horizontal scroll-through gallery, drag +
     swipe + click, dark near-black border, PROJECT NNN labels
  04 THE ARCHIVE — editorial filterable grid (placeholders)
  05 MEET HANA — magazine profile, PORTRAIT COMING SOON placeholder, bio
  06 WHAT DO YOU NEED? — interactive project finder (three steps)
  07 THE DIRECTORY — clean 5-column index of capabilities, hairlines
  08 FIELD NOTES — quieter editorial list
  09 SUNDAY STUDIES — curated experiments list
  10 SPECIAL PROJECTS — evolving list
  11 OFFICE HOURS — project inquiry form (SEND TO THE OFFICE)
  Footer — nav, file no, seals

**Asset plan** — NO generated photography. All imagery is an elegant
placeholder frame (a flat 2D brand motif: sun, porcelain fragment, floral
stem, cobalt scroll) slotted where the client's real photography goes. Logo,
sun mark (6 rays), 福祿壽 stamp, 創作 seal are hand-built SVG 2D graphics
(allowed by user). Favicon + OG card are crafted SVG.

**CTA inventory** (own interaction identity each, darken on click)
  VIEW PROJECT → (frame card) · SEND TO THE OFFICE → (form) ·
  the finder begin button · archive filter pills · nav links

**Division + structure** — thin hairline rules, small file-number labels,
section numbering (FILE NO. 001 …), precise columns, generous margins,
strong alignment, "T" intersection anchors per section, no cards-no-rounded
excess, no scrapbook props.
